How the game works

Three steps to
domination

01

Run or ride

Go to play.8me.in on your phone. Login as guest, Google or connect Strava. Tap Start Run — GPS tracks every step in real time.

02

Claim territory

Tap Finish when you stop. Your enclosed GPS route becomes owned territory on the live map. The further you run, the bigger your zone.

03

Defend & steal

Territory is never safe. Run through 50%+ of a rival's zone to steal it. Strike back, reclaim lost ground, and climb the leaderboard.

Questions

Got
questions?

Everything you need to know before your first run.

▶ Just start running
Do I need to create an account?
No — start playing instantly as a guest with just your name. Connect Strava to save your progress permanently and sync your runs automatically.
Is Infinite Me free?
Completely free. No ads, no in-app purchases, no premium tier. Just run and claim territory.
Do I need to download an app?
Not mandatory. Infinite Me runs entirely in your phone's browser at play.8me.in. Works on any modern Android or iPhone. You can also download the Android app for the best experience.
What happens when someone steals my zone?
You'll get an alert in the app, or enable notifications from your profile page in the browser. Simply run through the same area again to reclaim it — every zone can always be won back.
Can I use my Garmin or Apple Watch?
Yes! Export your run as a GPX file from any GPS watch and upload it directly in the app. Your entire run history can instantly become territory.
Does it work if I just walk?
Absolutely. Any GPS route counts — walk, jog, run, or cycle. Even a 1km walk becomes territory. The game is for everyone who moves.
