How the game works.

Start a run in the app at play.8me.in, or sync one from your watch. Every street, block and loop is up for grabs — from a daily 5K to a Sunday long ride, every metre counts.

Finish your activity and the ground you covered is claimed in your colour. Close loops, link streets and push into new areas to grow your control of the map — and take it from whoever held it before.

Territory is never safe. Rivals can take what's yours, and you can strike back — every claim moves the leaderboards, from your local area to the whole world. Keep moving or lose ground.
