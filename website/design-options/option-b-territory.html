Core mechanic

🗺️ Claiming territory

Run any route — loop, zigzag, straight line. The enclosed area becomes your owned zone.

Attack

⚔️ Stealing zones

Run through 50%+ of an opponent's zone to steal the whole thing. Precision matters.

Defense

Lost a zone? Run the same area again. Your new route creates fresh territory.
